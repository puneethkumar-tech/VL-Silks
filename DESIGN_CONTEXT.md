# VL Silks Design Context

## Brand Identity

VL Silks is a premium Indian garment/silk shop.

The visual identity should communicate:

* Trust
* Traditional Indian textile heritage
* Premium quality
* Modern retail
* Simplicity

Avoid making the interface look like a generic admin dashboard.

---

# Brand Name

VL SILKS

# Tagline

TRADITION MEETS FASHION

---

# Color Palette

Primary:
#8B0000

Secondary:
#D4AF37

Background:
#FFF8E1

Text:
#333333

Additional neutral colors may be used for borders, cards, disabled states and accessibility.

Do not randomly introduce bright colors.

---

# Visual Direction

The design should combine:

Traditional Indian luxury
+
Modern SaaS dashboard usability

Use:

* Maroon headers
* Gold accents
* Warm cream backgrounds
* Clean white content cards
* Subtle borders
* Moderate shadows
* Rounded but professional components
* Clear typography
* Plenty of spacing

Avoid:

* Excessive gradients
* Neon colors
* Excessive animations
* Glassmorphism everywhere
* Gaming-style UI
* Overly colorful dashboards

---

# Dashboard

The dashboard should prioritize business information.

Important information hierarchy:

1. Today's sales
2. Credit to collect
3. Stock
4. Purchases
5. Low stock
6. Recent activity

The Credit to Collect card should be visually noticeable because it is an important business metric.

# UX Principles

1. Important information should be visible without unnecessary navigation.

2. Forms should have clear labels.

3. Destructive actions require confirmation.

4. Success and error messages should be clear.

5. Tables should support search and filtering.

6. Currency should consistently use Indian Rupees.

7. Dates should use a consistent format.

8. The user should always know whether a transaction was saved successfully.

9. Avoid unnecessary popups.

10. Do not hide important business information behind multiple clicks.
